Regarding your first question related to payments - we have internal tool in which you will be able to complete monthly timesheet (what days you have worked, how many hours, if there are any absences itp) - this will need to be completed by the end of the month and then accepted by your Line Manager in the tool. Based on this timesheet you will receive payment each month.

You do not need to have any NIP number etc, all will be handled in our internal tool. Once you start you will be able to enter our HR portal to find instruction how to do it in the tool or you can always ask our Advisory Team for guidance.

Regarding second question - as you are EU citizen but you do not have PESEL number yet (it is personal identification number in Poland) the contracts were sent to you via courier as specified in the task. Once you receive it, sign one version, scan it and attach to the task. You can then arrange a courier to pick up one sign copy form you (also instruction will be attached together with contracts).

There will be separate task related to PESEL number in the candidate's portal (once you will come to Poland you will need to obtain it within few days and provide us with it. Sometimes they require to show contract that's why you will have signed by us hard copy).
